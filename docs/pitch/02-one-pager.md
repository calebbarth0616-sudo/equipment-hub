## The problem
Schools and families accumulate outgrown sports equipment that ends up in closets and landfills, while underserved schools and community leagues field teams in sneakers and shared gloves. The gear exists; the *connection* doesn't.

## What Equipment Hub does
A web platform where:
- **Donors** (schools, teams, individuals) list equipment with condition and quantity.
- **Schools and leagues** post specific needs with a short story of why.
- **Donors browse those requests** and offer their equipment directly. The team accepts, and the match is tracked through delivery.

Live now: https://equipment-hub-weld.vercel.app

## What we need from a logistics partner
Equipment Hub does the matching; it deliberately does not do the physical handoff. For each accepted match, the partner would:
1. Receive the donor's contact and the receiving team's contact (surfaced in-app once both sides agree).
2. Coordinate pickup or drop-off.
3. Confirm delivery (a single status update, which becomes the platform's public impact record).

Scale for the pilot: **a handful of matches per month** across 2–3 schools.

## Trust and safety (built in)
- Every school/league is **verified** before it can receive donations.
- No street addresses are stored; contact details are revealed only after a match is accepted.
- Donors are never shown publicly; schools are.
