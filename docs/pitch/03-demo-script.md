# 5-minute demo script

> Rehearse this twice end-to-end before the call. Have two browser windows
> ready BEFORE you start: normal window logged in as the DONOR, private
> window logged in as the ORG. Seed data should be visible on Browse.
> Timings are targets — total under 5 minutes leaves 15 for conversation.

## 0:00 — Frame it (30 sec)
"Let me show you the whole loop in about four minutes: a team asks, a donor offers, the team accepts, and that's the moment your team would step in."

## 0:30 — The team's side (60 sec)
*Private window (org account).*
- Land on **Browse** → "This is public; anyone can see what teams need right now."
- Point at a request card: the item, the quantity, and **the story** — "Requests with a real story get matched; that's the whole design."
- Click **My requests** → **+ Request equipment**: fill it in live (pick something the seed donor list can satisfy — e.g. Basketball, "Practice jerseys", 12, a one-line story). Post it.
- "Note the school's name appears publicly. Donors' names never do."

## 1:30 — The donor's side (60 sec)
*Normal window (donor account).*
- **Browse** → find the request you just posted → **"Offer one of my donations"**.
- Pick a donation from the dropdown → **Send offer**.
- "Three things just changed in the database at once: the offer exists, and both the donation and the request are marked as matched so nobody double-books them. That's atomic — it can't half-happen."

## 2:30 — The handoff moment (60 sec)
*Back to the private window (org).*
- Point at the amber badge on **Matches** → open it → the offer is at the top.
- Click **Accept** → status flips.
- Click **Show contact info** → the donor's name and email appear. "This is the moment that's for you. Both sides said yes; contact is unlocked; the equipment needs to physically move. Your team would pick it up from the donor and get it to the school."
- Point at the status labels: proposed → accepted → **with nonprofit** → **delivered**. "Those last two are yours to flip. Every delivered match becomes a public number."

## 3:30 — What's coming, briefly (45 sec)
- "By the pilot: schools get **verified** before they can receive anything; matching is **filtered by distance** so we're not pairing a donor in one state with a school in another; and **email notifications** so an athletic director doesn't have to check the site."
- "What I'm not building: shipping, payments, or messaging. Keep it simple; you handle the real world."

## 4:15 — Hand over the conversation (45 sec)
Ask, then stop talking:
1. "How do you currently move equipment or supplies — does this fit that, or fight it?"
2. "What would make you say no to this?"
3. "If we ran a September pilot with two or three schools you already know — who would they be?"

## Fallbacks
- **Wifi dies:** have screenshots of Browse, the offer panel, and the Matches page with contact revealed. Talk over them.
- **Something errors live:** say "that's a bug, I'll fix it tonight" and move to the next step. Never debug on a call.
- **They ask about cost:** "Free to schools and donors. Hosting is on free tiers for the pilot; sustainability is a conversation for after we've proven it works."
